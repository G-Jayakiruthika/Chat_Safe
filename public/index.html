07 // QUESTIONS

FAQ

Is my chat stored anywhere?

Your case files are saved only in your own browser. You can search, export or delete them. The server does not keep messages.

Does it work without the AI or the internet?

Yes. The offline rules engine works without an API key, and the browser-only version runs entirely on your device. Gemini adds deeper answers in every language when it's available.

Can it read my WhatsApp?

No, and it never asks to. You choose what to share: paste it, use the Android share menu, or forward it to the Chat_Safe WhatsApp Business number.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open Chat_Safe in Chrome, tap the menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". Then "Share → Chat_Safe" appears in WhatsApp and other apps.

How does Pay-Check know a UPI ID is safe?

Since 1 Oct 2025, SEBI-registered brokers, advisers and mutual funds collect money only through NPCI-issued "@valid" UPI handles. Chat_Safe checks the format and links to SEBI Check for confirmation.

I already sent money. What now?

Call 1930 immediately — the first hour matters most — then inform your bank and file at cybercrime.gov.in. Chat_Safe drafts the complaint for you.
